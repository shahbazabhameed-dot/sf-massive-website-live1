AVAILABLE SERVICE TYPES

Flexible support for
project logistics.

Vehicle and equipment selection is confirmed for each enquiry and is subject to availability, route conditions, and the agreed scope.

01

Lowbed transportation

Transport support for heavy machinery and equipment, planned around dimensions, loading requirements, route, and destination.

02

Flatbed transportation

Movement of structural items, construction materials, and general project cargo with suitable load planning and securing.

03

Dyna truck services

Transport support for smaller equipment, tools, materials, and time-sensitive site requirements.

04

Boom truck services

Transport and lifting support for suitable loads, with positioning and unloading requirements coordinated in advance.

05

Heavy equipment rental

Rental enquiries may include excavators, wheel loaders, skid-steer loaders, rollers, backhoes, or generators, subject to availability and requirements.

06

Site mobilization coordination

Coordinate equipment movement, delivery timing, access, offloading, and site handover requirements with project representatives.

DELIVERY COORDINATION

Plan the movement.
Control the handover.

Each request is reviewed against its practical transport and site requirements.

  • Confirm load or equipment details, origin, destination, and timing.
  • Review route, access, clearance, and site conditions.
  • Coordinate vehicle or equipment allocation and mobilization.
  • Agree loading, securing, offloading, and handover arrangements.
