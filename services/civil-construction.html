SCOPE OF SUPPORT

From ground preparation
to finishing works.

Final scope, sequence, materials, and interfaces are confirmed against each project’s drawings, specifications, and site conditions.

01

Pre-construction & site preparation

Site review, mobilization planning, setting-out coordination, clearing, excavation, grading, trenching, backfilling, and compaction.

02

Foundations & concrete

Foundation works, formwork, reinforcement, concrete placement, structural elements, plinths, and heavy-duty slabs.

03

Building & structural works

Building construction, masonry and blockwork, structural work, waterproofing, protective works, and architectural finishes.

04

Electrical installation support

Electrical installation coordination such as conduits, cabling, distribution, lighting, earthing, bonding, and inspection support, subject to the agreed project scope.

05

External & infrastructure works

Site development, paving, drainage, utility trenches, manholes, boundary works, and related civil interfaces.

06

Renovation & modifications

Repair, refurbishment, and modification works planned around access, existing conditions, and project controls.

HOW WE WORK

Plan. Prepare.
Execute. Inspect.

Work planning and delivery are coordinated with the client, consultant, and other contractors as required by the assignment.

  • Review scope, drawings, specifications, and site conditions.
  • Coordinate work sequence, resources, and interfaces.
  • Follow applicable site safety and quality requirements.
  • Coordinate inspections, records, and handover activities.
