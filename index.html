01

CIVIL · BUILDING · INFRASTRUCTURE

Civil Construction, Building & Infrastructure

Potential scopes span pre-construction review and mobilization; surveying, clearing, excavation, grading, trenching, filling and compaction; foundations, reinforced concrete, formwork and steel fixing; structural and building works; masonry, waterproofing, painting, ceilings and finishes; external development, paving, drainage and utility trenches; industrial foundations and repairs; plus electrical installation support as part of the civil division.

Electrical activities may include conduits and trunking, power/control cabling, distribution systems and lighting, earthing and bonding, inspection and testing support, coordinated with approved documents and assigned responsibilities.

Explore civil construction →
02

TRANSPORT · EQUIPMENT

Transportation & Heavy Equipment Rental

Transport enquiries may cover lowbed movements for suitable machinery, flatbed cargo, Dyna truck deliveries, and boom truck transport/lifting subject to capacity and site conditions. Rental enquiries may include wheel loaders, crawler or wheeled excavators, skid-steer loaders, rollers, backhoes and generators, subject to availability and agreed terms.

Planning can include scope and load review, route/access checks, vehicle or plant allocation, pre-dispatch inspection, loading and securing coordination, journey updates, and offloading and handover.

Explore transport & rental →
03

EOT CRANE SYSTEMS

EOT Crane Installation & Commissioning

Installation support may include site and runway readiness review; mobilization and work-area preparation; rail positioning, alignment, specified welding and inspection coordination; bridge, end carriage, trolley and hoist assembly; conductor bars or festoon, power/control cabling, panels, pendant/radio controls, earthing and bonding; functional checks of applicable limits and safety systems; testing, commissioning coordination, records and handover.

Crane types and commissioning responsibilities are confirmed against the manufacturer’s documentation, approved project scope, required specialist parties and applicable procedures.

Explore crane installation →
04

FIRE PROTECTION

Firefighting Systems

Depending on approved drawings and the agreed scope, potential support can include firefighting pipework, supports and fittings; sprinkler installation coordination; hose cabinets, reels and hydrant interfaces; fire pump room and fire-water supply connections; fire points and extinguisher installation; and interfaces with fire alarm and electrical specialists.

Design, authority approvals, licensing, specialist testing, commissioning and certification must be confirmed with appropriately authorized parties for each project. This page does not represent a claim of specific approval or certification.
